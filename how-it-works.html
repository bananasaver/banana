How It Works

Finding the deals

Banana Saver looks across technology and gaming retailers and product sources to identify prices, offers and products worth considering. As the site develops, more of this checking will be automated so prices can be refreshed regularly.

What you see on Banana

  • Deal of the Day — a featured deal selected for attention.
  • Latest Deals — products and offers across our technology categories.
  • Latest Tech Reviews — hands-on and researched reviews of interesting products.
  • Tech Rankings by Bananas — focused comparisons between specific competing products.

Prices can move

Retail prices, stock and promotions can change. A price shown on Banana should always be checked on the retailer's site before you buy.

How Banana makes money

Banana Saver may earn commission from some retailer links. We may also develop direct commercial relationships with retailers and manufacturers.
