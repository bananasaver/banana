What we actually look at

Buying technology can mean comparing a dozen specifications that sound important but don't necessarily change your everyday experience. Banana reviews focus on the features that matter in real use, how a product compares with alternatives and whether the price makes sense.

Value matters

A great product at the wrong price isn't necessarily a great deal. That's why the review system and the deal system are connected but separate: the review asks whether the product is good; the deal asks whether today's price is good.
